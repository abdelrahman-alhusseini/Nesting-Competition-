V14.1 Activity Feed Fix
=======================

Agent-facing Activity Feed now shows card/game activity only.

Shown event types:
- card_drawn
- special_card_drawn
- even_card_resolved
- special_card_saved
- special_card_expired
- future card_used/card_applied/special_card_used/special_card_applied events

Hidden from the agent Activity Feed:
- booking_submitted
- booking_approved
- booking_rejected
- booking_reversed
- manual_draw_granted
- points_adjusted
- unrelated system/audit events

Player names are loaded from public.profiles through activity_events.actor_id.
The admin dashboard's full activity count remains unchanged.
