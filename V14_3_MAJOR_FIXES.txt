Michael & Son Nesting Champions — V14.3 Major Gameplay Fixes

1. ADMIN LEADERBOARD
- Admin Adjust Points leaderboard now renders the complete leaderboard in a scrollable table instead of truncating the player list.

2. PENDING DRAWS — 24 HOUR WINDOW
- Approving multiple bookings no longer discards older pending draws because of a count limit.
- Every approved/manual draw gets its own 24-hour expiry timestamp.
- Expired draws are cleaned up automatically when the agent refreshes/loads pending draws.
- The Draw Cards page shows how long the oldest available draw has left.

3. SPECIAL CARD USE
- Use Card now opens an in-place Flutter dialog instead of navigating the player to a separate special-card page.
- Targeted cards let the player choose the target directly from the live leaderboard.
- Supported targeted cards: Move Another Player Down 2 Ranks, Transfer 5 Points, Rank Swap.
- Self cards apply immediately to the current player.

4. SHIELD
- Shield can be activated on yourself.
- It is one-use and automatically blocks the next negative effect.
- It blocks targeted special-card attacks, Reverse/-1 results, and a -6 even-number gamble loss.
- After blocking one negative effect the Shield charge is consumed.
